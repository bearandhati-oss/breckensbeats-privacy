Brecken's Beats - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Brecken's Beats ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles information.

Information We Do Not Collect

Brecken's Beats does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not collect names, email addresses, location data, device identifiers, or any other personal data.

No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising

Brecken's Beats contains no third-party analytics, no advertising networks, and no tracking of any kind.

Audio Recordings

The app uses your device's microphone to record voice and beatbox sounds. All recordings are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to any server.

In-App Purchases

Brecken's Beats offers an optional subscription (Brecken's Beats Plus) processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Children's Privacy

Brecken's Beats is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else. The app fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
